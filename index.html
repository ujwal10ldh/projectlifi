THE IDEA

About Our Project

Hamare model mein mobile phone se audio signal AUX cable ke through transmitter circuit tak pahunchta hai. Transmitter circuit LED ki brightness ko audio signal ke according change karta hai. LED se nikalne wali changing light receiver ke solar panel par padti hai. Solar panel light changes ko electrical signal mein convert karta hai. Receiver se signal AUX cable ke through speaker tak jata hai, jahan music sunai deta hai.

🎯

Project Objective

To demonstrate the basic idea of transmitting an audio signal using changes in LED light and receiving it with a light-sensitive panel.

🔬

What This Model Demonstrates

This is an educational light-based audio transmission model. It is not a complete commercial Li-Fi internet or networking system.

FROM SOUND TO LIGHT AND BACK

How Our Project Works

The system sends a changing electrical audio signal through a light source, then converts the received light variations back into an electrical signal.

STEP 01
📱

Audio Input

Mobile phone se music audio output/AUX connection ke through transmitter circuit tak aata hai.

STEP 02
🔌

Transmitter Circuit

Circuit audio signal ke according LED ki light intensity ko modulate karta hai.

STEP 03
💡

Light Transmission

LED ki changing light receiver ki direction mein travel karti hai. Transmitter aur receiver ki alignment zaroori hai.

STEP 04
🔆

Solar Panel Receiver

Solar panel light mein hone wale changes ko electrical signal mein convert karta hai.

STEP 05
🔊

Audio Output

Receiver circuit aur AUX connection ke through signal speaker tak pahunchta hai, aur music sunai deta hai.

Mobile Phone→AUX Cable→Transmitter Circuit→LED Light→Solar Panel Receiver→Receiver Circuit / AUX→Speaker

THE SCIENCE

Science Behind Li-Fi

The core idea is to encode information in changing light and detect those changes at the receiver.

〰️

Modulation

Light intensity can be changed in a pattern that represents information. This is called modulation.

🔄

Light to Electricity

A light-sensitive receiver converts changes in received light into electrical changes.

🎚️

Signal Quality

LED type, receiver, circuit design, alignment, ambient light and distance can affect performance.

Our model demonstrates a basic principle of light-based signal transmission. Commercial Li-Fi systems require more advanced hardware, signal processing and networking technology.
A BALANCED VIEW

Advantages & Limitations

✅

Advantages

  • Demonstrates an alternative way to transmit information.
  • Shows light as a transmission medium in this model.
  • Helps students learn electronics, circuits and communication.
  • Makes science learning interactive.
  • Can inspire further research into optical communication.
⚠️

Limitations

  • The receiver needs sufficient light from the transmitter.
  • Alignment and distance can affect the result.
  • Ambient light may interfere with the signal.
  • Audio quality depends on the circuit and components.
  • This basic model does not provide internet or full Li-Fi networking.

GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. What is Li-Fi?

Li-Fi is a light-based wireless communication technology that can transmit information using light.

Q2. Does our model provide internet?

No. Our model is a basic educational demonstration of transmitting an audio signal using light.

Q3. What does the solar panel do?

The solar panel helps convert changes in light into an electrical signal.

Q4. Must the light beam visibly flicker?

No. The information is represented by changes in light intensity, which can happen too quickly for the eye to see as flicker.

Q5. Does the website simulation control the real model?

No. It is only a visual explanation and does not control the hardware or transmit real audio.
